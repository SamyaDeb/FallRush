# G4.6 — Candy palette + grading (2026-11-20, agent `palette`)

Render only. Map content hashes, colliders, hazard timing and rules are unchanged (all six scenes rebuilt by `AllMapsBuilder.Run`: every recorded hash reproduced, `Tests/Vectors/map_hashes_v1.txt` untouched). No WebGL build, no Mac dev server build, no VPS.

## 1. What changed

- **Palette tokens** (`Scripts/Net/PaletteTokens.cs`, doc 17 §3.1): six surface hues (top + side). The meaning colours were updated to the plan's values: hit pink `#FF2D8A`, lemon `#FFD43B`, mint `#2FE0A8`, goo `#9B3CFF`, and sky `#2E9BFF → #A6E3FF`. The section table is `PaletteTokens.Sections`, with the hue per section and whether a yellow telegraph shows there. The light tokens are a warm sun `#FFF1DC`, a cool sky ambient `#BFD9FF` and a warm violet ground `#C2A6D6`.
- **Section tags from the builders:**
  - `BlockPartyDashBuilder.SectionAt(pos)`: by z, from the layout constants.
  - `GooTowerBuilder.SectionAt(t)`: from the builder's `S{k}_` / `Corner_{k}` / `gt_k{k}` names.
  - `StarScrambleBuilder.SectionAt(t)`: from the builder's names.
  - The lobby's `Tile_x_z` parity rule is in `MapArt`, so `LobbyBuilder` is untouched.
  - `MapArt.StyleFor` picks the top, band and side colours from the section hue.
  - Stripes stay where they were. Goals, springs, spawns and checkpoints stay mint.
  - Rails and walls take the section hue.
  - Belts take the hue at S 0.5 / V 0.78 under the light chevrons.
  - Turntable stripes and moving-platform rims take the hue darkened toward ink, so mint never sits on a walkable top.
  - Machine parts (the Grape material) are Grape deepened toward ink, `#5F25BD`.
  - Round floors and discs (static prisms) are now vertex-coloured on the shared material. The Star Fountain floor left its own material and merged into the static chunks.
- **The bake** (decision G4.6-3): every vertex colour goes through `MapArt.Bake` = `GraphicsQuality.AlbedoFor(token, map light)`. This is the exact inverse of the grade (described below) divided by the light on a lit top, so a top shows its token on screen. The same bake applies to:
  - the merged hazards;
  - the dressing primitives;
  - the Lid Flip lid materials.

  The G2 tops read near-white for two reasons: the project renders in linear space and vertex colours are not converted, and the old sun rim was on tops as well.
- **Grade** (`Client/GraphicsQuality.cs`):
  - Neutral tonemapping. This needs URP's HDR grading mode; `Apply` switches it at runtime and the asset on disk stays LDR.
  - Saturation +22, contrast +12, post-exposure −0.15.
  - A ×1.6 pre-tonemap gain (the colour filter) on both levels.
  - High adds bloom (threshold 1.15) and the vignette. Low drops only those two and keeps the 0.8 render scale.
- **Toon shader**:
  - `_ViewRim` + `_RimAll`: a view rim that does not depend on the sun. It is on for Rushlings (the Character material) and the stripe posts, and on merged hit hazards via uv2.y = 4. Static bumpers keep their uv2 through `MeshBuilder`.
  - Both the sun rim and the view rim are off on up-facing faces.
  - No new keywords; two halfs were added to `UnityPerMaterial`.
- **Lid Flip** (`LidGridView`, colours only):
  - Ring 4, and rings 1–0, are Bubblegum (decision G4.6-5). Ring 3 is Tangerine and ring 2 is Soda blue.
  - The lower shelf is Grape fizz, with a Grape-fizz-side outer ring.
  - Lid tops lost the gloss glint.
  - Five calm batches, as before. Rings 4 and 1–0 share one.
- **Tools:** `Editor/PaletteShots.cs` (the shots, the measurements, the colourblind sheets, the swatch sheet and the post-cost proxy).

## 3. Measured tops after the grade (screen pixels)

**Method:** `PaletteShots`, Editor, quality level 0 (the WebGL Mobile pipeline asset), the runtime volume at High, and the client's ambient globals.

- A raycast finds the walkable top under each sample point.
- A camera 2.5 m away at about 60° down (off the gloss angle) renders it.
- The value is the median of a 9 × 9 patch.

Full tables: `g4/palette-report-before.md`, `g4/palette-report-after.md`.

## 9. Decisions (also in doc 15)

- **G4.6-1** The top tokens are the plan hues brought into the band the brief asks for. The plan hexes have V 1.0, and Lemon / Tangerine have S 0.77 / 0.76, so they are taken to V 0.92 and S ≤ 0.74; Sour apple already fits. The sides are the plan's values.
- **G4.6-2** The meaning tokens now carry the plan values: hit pink, lemon, mint, goo and sky. The brand tokens (Magenta, Sun Yellow, Grape) are unchanged for the UI.
- **G4.6-3** Palette colours are on-screen colours. The map bake inverts the grade and the light, and the pre-tonemap gain is ×1.6.
  - Neutral maps scene white to sRGB 0.82, and saturation +22 drove linear-baked tokens to S 1.0 with up to 12° of hue shift (first pass, measured).
  - Post-exposure stays −0.15 as specified. The gain is what keeps the tops in V 0.85–0.95.
  - Bloom threshold 1.15: a lit top sits at about 1.0 in the scene buffer, so it does not bloom.
- **G4.6-4** Low keeps the grade (see §7). The HDR grading mode is set at runtime.
- **G4.6-5** Lid Flip rings 1–0: Sour apple → Bubblegum. Sour apple was within 0.06 luma of the lit lemon. Rings 4 and 1–0 share a batch, so the lid draws stay at 5 calm batches.
- **G4.6-6** Mint is reserved:
  - moving-platform rims and turntable spokes / stripes became the section hue darkened toward ink, so movers read a shade darker than the static tops;
  - the Grape material was deepened toward ink, because the light grape top sat ΔE 9 from Grape fizz on Goo Tower's mallet arms.
- **G4.6-7** View rim: on Rushlings, stripe posts and hit hazards; off on tops. The sun rim is also off on tops. Outline tiers (thick on hazards, thin on props) are **not done**.
- **G4.6-8** The sky is not compensated: it reads cooler and greyer after the grade. The menu cameras run no post, so a compensated sky material would over-brighten the menu. This is a look call.
- **G4.6-9** The Sweep Pad stays Bubblegum with the pink / yellow sweeper bars. The rule passes (the stripe's yellow band vs the floor: 0.29 luma), but the bars' pink half merges into the floor. The fallback is to move the Sweep Pad to another hue. Your call.
- **G4.6-10** The Goo Tower falling blocks (walkable) stay hazard-striped yellow / magenta, as built in G4.3. They are hazards, so they are outside the hue rule.
