## B. "The floor getting black and somehow broken" — fixed
**Reproduced in the Mac player** (new dev tool `scripts/g413-lidview.sh`: a one-round Lid Flip on the Mac dev server, one
windowed probe client, a frame every 1–2 s; Editor shots cannot show it, `LidGridView.Snapshot()` turns shadows off). Seen in
**every** look and level: soft High, soft Low (from the lower shelf, looking up), candy High.

Suspects checked, in the prompt's order:
1. **Shadows on the instanced lids: not the cause.** Soft High with lid shadows off (a temporary dev switch, removed before
   the commit) shows the same black slabs and shards; candy (no lid shadows) shows them too.
2. **Open colour too dark: a cause.** The open lid measured on screen (82, 61, 122) = exactly the `Open` token (luma 0.27);
   its shaded / underside faces (48, 30, 83), luma 0.15. Beside the pastel lids (luma ≈ 0.85) that reads as black.
3. **Geometry: the cause of "broken".** The lid swung about its **top** hinge edge, so its 0.4 m body swept 0.34 m into the
   neighbour lid (the gap is 6 cm). Hidden inside the neighbour except at its rounded corners, where dark wedges / shards poked
   out during the swing (the jagged black triangles).
4. Sorting / z-fighting: nothing beyond 3.

Fix (render only; colliders and hazard timing untouched, map hashes unchanged):
- the hinge is the lid's **bottom** edge facing the centre: the swung lid stays inside its own hole at every angle;
- soft: the open lid is a **dark lavender #7E6BB4** (luma 0.46; underside ≈ 0.28) and keeps 60 % of the sun in a cast shadow
  (it hangs in its neighbours' shadow); candy keeps its colour (only the hinge changes there). Lit / warn telegraph unchanged.
- tests: `LidFlipTests.FlippedLid_StaysInsideItsOwnHole_EveryAngle` (every lid, 0–90°, both grids), `PaletteTests.
  LidFlip_OpenLid_DarkLavender_NotBlack`.

## C. Saturated Lid Flip lids (soft) — done, within the readability rules (G4.13-2)
The prompt's start band (S 0.45–0.60, V 0.88–0.95, same hues) **cannot pass** the hard rules: the soft lit amber #FF8A00 has
luma 0.60, and Bubblegum / Tangerine / Soda blue / Grape at that S and V land at luma 0.55–0.72 — inside the ±0.15 band (only
Grape at S 0.6 passes). Lighter (luma ≥ 0.75) is only reachable at lower S; darker (≤ 0.45) needs V ≈ 0.6–0.7 (a dark floor,
the opposite of B, and a raspberry ring would match the warn magenta's luma). So each ring got the **most saturation the rules
allow at V 1.0**, same hue (option (a), told to the user; reversible):

| ring | hue | before (soft pastel) | after (`PaletteTokens.SoftLidHex`) | lit gap normal / deutan / protan | in cast shadow |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4, 1–0 | Bubblegum | #F9D1E2 S 0.16 | **#FFC2DC S 0.24** | 0.219 / 0.171 / 0.232 | 0.184 / 0.135 / 0.197 |
| 3 | Tangerine | #F9E4D1 S 0.16 | **#FFC894 S 0.42** | 0.215 / 0.168 / 0.220 | 0.180 / 0.132 / 0.186 |
| 2 | Soda blue | #D6EDF9 S 0.14 | **#D1EFFF S 0.18** (bluer is ΔE < 15 to the soft sky horizon) | 0.317 / 0.240 / 0.360 | 0.278 / 0.201 / 0.320 |
| lower | Grape fizz | #DED1F9 S 0.16 | **#DECFFF S 0.19** | 0.237 / 0.167 / 0.265 | 0.201 / 0.131 / 0.230 |

(gaps computed with the test's maths: sRGB luma, Machado 2009 severity 1; "in cast shadow" = the sun term at
`CalmShadowKeep` 0.85. The lower edge ring keeps its G4.8z deep tone.) Rules held and tested
(`PaletteTests.LidFlip_SoftLids_SaturatedSet_SameHues_LumaGapsHoldInShadow`): ≥ 0.15 normal, ≥ 0.15 deutan / protan
unshadowed (the old rule, not weakened), ≥ 0.12 in a cast shadow, warn magenta ≥ 0.15 off every calm lid, ΔE ≥ 15 off mint,
lemon, goo, hit pink and the sky. Other maps keep `SoftTopHex`; candy unchanged.
