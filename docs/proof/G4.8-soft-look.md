## 1. What changed
| part | what | where |
|---|---|---|
| Look switch | `soft` (default) / `candy` (G4.6, unchanged). `?look=soft\|candy` (WebGL), `-fallrushLook` (desktop), **F7** in game. One geometry set with two colour channels: vertex COLOR = soft albedo, uv3 = (candy albedo, 2 + pattern + 8 × role); the toon shader picks per vertex from the global `_FR_Look`. Grade, sun, ambient, fog, sky, shadows and the backdrop follow the look. | `LookState`, `LookOnly`, `Toon.shader`, `GraphicsQuality` |
| Soft palette | Pastel walkable tops (S ≈ 0.16, V 0.975), mid-tone sides (S 0.45, V 0.86), saturated **coloured rims** in the plan hues; soft sky `#66B5F2 → #9CD2F5` + haze `#DDEFFB`; warm sun `#FFF3E0`; lavender-tinted ambient ground. Meaning colours (hazard, goal, boost, lid lit) stay saturated and never sit on a top. | `PaletteTokens`, `Palette.asset`, doc 17 §3.0 |
| Grade | Soft: no tonemapper, saturation +6, contrast +8, exposure 0, no gain, bloom threshold 1.25. Candy: G4.6 values. The inverse-grade bake is per look, so the screen shows the token. | `GraphicsQuality.GradeOf` |
| Bevels | Every static block is a rounded box (3 segments when r ≥ 0.15, per-axis ellipsoidal so thin slabs stay round); props ≥ 0.14 m radius. | `ToyMesh.RoundBox`, `MapArt` |
| Padded rims | A tube (r 0.12) along each **exposed** edge run of a walkable block ≥ 0.9 m wide: crown + 0.08 m, outer + 0.09 m (≤ 0.15 m beyond the collider); runs that touch a neighbour (probe) are skipped, so a rim never fills a gap. | `ToyMesh.RimRing`, `MapArt.BlockShape` |
| Chunky | A walkable block under 0.6 m is drawn down to 0.6 m only if a 2 m box under it is empty. Never above or beyond the collider top. | `MapArt` |
| Patterns | One per hue: Soda blue / Lemon stripes, Tangerine / Grape dots, Sour apple checks, Bubblegum chevrons; ±5 % value, world space, fwidth AA; never on belts, movers or hazards. | `Toon.shader Pattern()` |
| Foam shading | Wide soft bands, half-Lambert blend, gloss on rims / hazards / props, sky-tinted Fresnel. | `Toon.shader` |
| Shadows | URP main light, soft look only: 1 cascade, 60 m, soft, 2048 High / 1024 Low, strength 0.6; full shadow keeps 25 % of the sun term (a lavender patch, never black). Rushlings cast and receive. | `Mobile_RPAsset`, `GraphicsQuality.ApplyShadows`, `MapArt.ShadowCasters` |
| Sky + backdrop | Sky shader reads look globals; soft clouds (own seed) and a low stadium backdrop (14 arcs, 4 tiers, towers, banners; unlit) 260 m beyond the course, at the horizon band. Soft look only (`LookOnly`); G4.6 clouds stay candy-only. | `SkyGradient.shader`, `MapDressing` |

A/B** (§9): if the lit lid is ever missed in soft, the fix is a darker shaded top ring (render only).

## 8. Originality (per map)
All geometry is code-built from primitives (rounded boxes, tubes, prisms, discs) by our builders; patterns are procedural in
our shader; colours are our tokens. No asset was extracted, ripped or traced.
- **Capsule Run:** own layout (G4.2); rims / bevels / patterns generated from its colliders.
- **Goo Tower:** own spiral tower (G4.3); same generator.
- **Star Fountain:** own fountain arena (G4.4); same generator; the fountain cake is ours.
- **Lid Flip:** own lid grid (G4.5); lid colours from our tokens.
- **Lobby / Sandbox:** same generator.
- **Backdrop:** a generic low arc stadium made of our boxes; no reference image was used.
