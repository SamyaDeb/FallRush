## 7. Decisions and deviations (doc 15 G4.5-n)

- **G4.5-1** Lid view is instanced (`LidGridView`); the hinge swing is 90° down (a trapdoor) rather than a 180° flip-over: a 180° turn about an edge would lay the lid on its neighbour.
- **G4.5-2** Open time 45 → **30 ticks** and the shortest wave interval 45 → **60**: with lit + open > interval, a lid can be picked again while still open, close already yellow and flip after ≈ 15 ticks — under the 1 s telegraph. Lit + open ≤ interval removes that (test `EveryFlip_FollowsAFullSecondOfYellow`). The lid maths is unchanged; this is def data.
- **G4.5-3** Stage shares 16 → 50 % (A) / 18 → 50 % (B) and the lower shelf from 20 s, 35 → 50 % (tuning, §3).
- **G4.5-4** **12 spawns** on ring 2, not 3: spawns are per slot (`SimCore` needs 12) and the 3 finalists can be any slots; slots 0–2 are 120° apart. Compacting finalists onto 3 spawns would change shared spawn code for every map.
- **G4.5-5** The sweeper hangs from the claw (cable + hub, no post): every lid including the centre one flips, so a post would float; the hub is inside the bar's knock volume, so touching it is a knock (collision = visuals).
- **G4.5-6** Bot-side tuning ends rounds: misjudgement (late notice of the own lid), a panic hop, and **+20 points late-game** misjudgement after the last ring retires. The map alone (fair 1 s telegraph, ≥ 5 inner lids solid) never forces a fall.
- **G4.5-7** Normal medians 76.5–83.4 s: inside 60–100, the 80–95 aim met in 2 of 6 cells (pushing them up cost the second-chance target).
- **G4.5-8 (your call)** **The timer tiebreak on a lid map.** `FinalRules` ranks timer survivors by the deepest *crumble* shelf reached, then fewer tiles touched, then slot. On Lid Flip there is no crumble grid, so both are 0 for everyone and **a timer ending is decided by slot**. Normal bots reach the timer in 0–3 % of rounds, flawless reference bots in 29–57 %; real players will be in between. Not changed (rules frozen, prompt rule 4). Options: (a) leave it (rare with real mistakes); (b) a small `FinalRules` change: on lid maps, "stayed on the top shelf" beats "on the lower shelf", then the fewest falls, then slot (a rules change; ruleset_v1 untouched, needs a test); (c) both.
- **G4.5-9** The probe client's bot (`RaceProbeBot`, dev only) plays the Final with `BotBrain`.

## 9. The VRF link (seed → pattern)

Each lid grid's state at hazard tick t is `HazardMath.LidStateAt(def, lid, t)` with `def.Seed = ObstaclePhase.Hash(hazardSeed, "cs_lids_top" | "cs_lids_low")` (`HazardLidGrid.Resolve` → `HazardResolve.SetSeed`); a wave's lids are the first k of a SplitMix64 partial Fisher–Yates of the alive lids keyed by `seed ⊕ wave` (`LidWaveSet`). Integers only, no replication. In M6 `hazardSeed` comes from the round's VRF output, so the Final's whole flip pattern is decided on chain. Test `HazardSeed_DecidesTheLidPattern`.
