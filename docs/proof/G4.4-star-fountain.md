**Burst view (view only):** `Shared/Hazards/StarBurst.cs` — `NextWave`, `CrownGlow(t)` (ramps over the second before a wave, flashes at the spawn) and `ArcPosMm(id, t)` (crown → the anchor's pose **at the spawn tick**, a 2.5 m arc, over the last 15 ticks). Pure functions of the hazard time, no allocation. `ReactiveViews` draws the arc stars (with a 2-star trail) through the existing instanced star batch (one pool of 64 matrices, no per-frame allocation) and a crown-glow sphere; the DEV practice mode now hands its local hazard clock to `ReactiveViews`, so the stars and the burst show there too (`PracticeSource`). `SkillRules`, the star timeline and the pickup / drop maths are unchanged; hazard vectors v1 `fc06826c` / v2 `d4b2a73a` / v3 `e158996b` unchanged (their tests pass).

**Dressing:** `MapDressing.StarScramble` (drum base under the arena, balloons at r 32, islands far below) is outside every gameplay volume and nothing sits between a spring and its island; the M4 record grooves on the table were dropped for the builder's radial stripes.

**Bots (G4.4b, `Shared/BotBrain.cs`, Skill only):**
- the burst: when the crown glows (a wave within 1.5 s) and the current target is more than 4 m away, head for the fountain ring (r 5 m on your side); once the arcs fly (the last 0.5 s, what a player sees) the next wave's stars are targets at their spawn-tick pose;
- ride the table: aim at a star's pose at t + travel time (≤ 1 s);
- the sweeper bars: the same look-ahead jump reflex as the Final's bars (`ReactToBars`);
- golden stars through a spring, within the tier's `StarPickRange` (island stars are found by their anchor's island, not by height — at 1.4 m they never passed the old 1.8 m test): wait 2.5 m short of the pad until the island will be under the ballistic landing point, run over the pad, keep steering at that island in the air (**flight lock**), within 30° of the flight direction; hop back off the island's inner edge at 30° off its line;
- steer round springs you are not using; no MoonBounce 70 % air stick in Skill (it braked spring flights).
Tiers keep their `StarPickRange` / reaction / speed differences (normal bots drop more stars than reference bots: 1.20–1.76 vs 0.89–1.31).
