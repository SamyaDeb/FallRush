- **Repo clutter** moved to `docs/archive/`: agent prompts, the v1 spec, the v1 engineering report, the design canvas. The logo is now `docs/media/logo.png`.
- **Judge mode** (`/play?judge=1`):
  - `POST /api/judge` reserves an idle on-chain arena as a private room flagged `judge`. The Classic overflow server or the other arena stays public. Limited to 4 per IP per hour; `JUDGE_ON=0` turns it off.
  - The game server starts the room's first match as soon as the judge is in. It fast-forwards rounds 1–3: the humans are ranked first, the bots by `sha256("fallrush-judge", seed, round, slot)`, and the results go out as the usual transcript events. The Final is played on the rollup. Later matches in the room wait for the host's Start, so an idle tab cannot loop matches.
  - The handoff waits up to 45 s, instead of 8 s, for the keeper.
  - The keeper skips seeding the qualify markets and skips the Season record. The receipt labels the rounds "fast-forwarded (judge mode)".
  - A QR card links to `/live/<arena>` on a phone. `/live` also shows a phone QR on desktop.
  - Default matches are unchanged. EditMode: 474 run, 473 passed, 0 failed, 1 skipped. That includes 3 new `JudgeModeTests`.
- **Receipt:** TEE txs are no longer linked to the devnet cluster; they are labelled "private rollup".
- **Hosting:** the keeper honours `SOLANA_RPC_URL`, ready for a private devnet RPC. The public endpoint stays until a key is added.
- **PROOF.md:** generated by `tools/p7/proof-links.ts` + `tools/p7/proof-md.mjs`. Fixes in this run:
  - bets and drops are picked by their econ op;
  - the tick is sampled across the whole Final;
  - the VRF callback is found before `start_final`;
  - the drop is found via the buyer's chips account;
  - version-1 transactions are read over raw JSON-RPC.
