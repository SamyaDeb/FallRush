## 1. Plan (as posted, then followed)
- **Lid schedule:** ring = Chebyshev distance from the centre; outer rings retire at `RingRetireTicks` (outermost first, warn 90 ticks before), the inner 3 × 3 never. Wave starts are a closed-form sum over the stages (≤ 4 terms). Wave w lights the first ⌊alive·‰⌋ lids of a SplitMix64 partial Fisher–Yates over the lids alive for the whole wave window (so a lid never lights while its ring is warned or retired). Fairness inside the function: each wave keeps at most ⌊(9 − MinInnerSolid) / ⌈Flipped / min interval⌉⌋ inner lids.
- **Posed lid colliders:** `PhysxMotorWorld.BeginTick(t)` switches each lid collider from the pure state, like a door is posed. Rewind / replay calls it per tick.
- **Ball contact:** in `ApplyKnocks` after spinners and mallets, before bumpers.
- **Bump rule:** inside `SimCore.Separate`'s slot-ordered pair loop; prediction applies it to the local player vs remotes.
- **Vectors v3:** v1 + v2 unchanged, then the G4 cases.

## 6. Decisions and deviations
1. **Views:** the prompt asked for a `HazardRollingBall` view MonoBehaviour and a `HazardLidGrid` view. Here the MonoBehaviours (one per file) are the authoring components and hold the visual transforms, and the posing happens in `Client/HazardViewDriver.cs`, as it does for every other hazard. Same effect: render only, from the pure pose.
2. **Lid schedule:** fully closed form (no precomputed table). Fairness is a per-wave cap on inner lids, derived from the worst-case overlap of flipped windows (4 per wave with the default timing). That is stronger and simpler than re-checking overlapping waves, and it is tested at every tick, also with half the lids per wave and overlapping windows.
3. **Lids never light while their ring is warned or retired:** a lid's wave membership requires it to stay un-warned for the whole wave window.
4. **`LoopTicks` added to the lid def** (0 = off, the default): the free-play sandbox runs hazard time from server start, so without it the rings would retire once and never come back. Rounds never loop.
5. **Ball push:** roll direction + ½ × the normalised lateral offset (an off-centre hit also shoves you sideways into the crowd, doc 08 rule 4.4).
6. **Free route = no knock hit and no door push-out.** Falls are counted and shown but do not make a route "not free" (falling off by yourself is not a hazard). A door wait has no contact, so the S4 doors also show as free.
7. **Bump stagger reuses the G1 stumble:** 55 % speed (not the G4 prompt's 60 %), 8 ticks (⌈250 ms⌉), no new state, snapshot unchanged, no protocol change.
8. **Display names:** the in-world title signs built into the map scenes ("BLOCK PARTY DASH" etc.) are map geometry. They change with the G4.2–G4.5 rebuilds, which remove all in-world labels. This step changed only on-screen UI.
9. **The prediction twin** for the G4 hazards uses the real `Map_Sandbox` scene's resolved ball and lid defs on analytic geometry, like the M3 kit twin. Two motor worlds must not share one set of PhysX colliders in one process. The lid toggle also compares against `collider.enabled` itself, to be safe.
