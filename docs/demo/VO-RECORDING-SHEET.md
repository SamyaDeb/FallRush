# Fall Rush: the founder pitch (voice-over for the 4:00 demo)

You're the founder, walking a judge through your product while it plays. You built this and you're proud of it. Talk to one person, not a crowd.

**How to read the marks**
- `/` is a short pause (about 0.3 s).
- `//` is a long pause (about 0.8 s).
- CAPS means stress that word.
- About 2 words a second, slower than feels natural.

> **Locked choices (26 Nov):** the Chaos Drop and the sealed pick are proven in a full on-chain match, so L6 is the full version. No human has won a Final on camera, so L8 is the fallback. If your beta session gets a human Crown, read the alternative and send me the match id. Numbers on screen are never spoken.

## Before you start
- Find a quiet room. A closet, or a blanket over your head, kills the echo.
- Hold the phone about 20 cm from your mouth, a little to the side.
- **Record 10 seconds of silence first** (room tone).
- Do 3 takes per line. Send one file per line, or one long file with 3 s gaps, as WAV or M4A.
- Smile on L1, L6, L7-out and L12. People can hear it.
