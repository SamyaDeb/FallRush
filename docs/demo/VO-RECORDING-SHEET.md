## Before you start
- Use a quiet room. A closet or a blanket over your head kills the echo.
- Hold the phone about 20 cm from your mouth, a little off to the side.
- **Record 10 seconds of silence first** (room tone).
- One file per line is best, or one long file with 3 s gaps. Send WAV or M4A.
- Smile on lines 1, 7-out and 12. People can hear it.
