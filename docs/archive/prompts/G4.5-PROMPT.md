Your task is **G4.5: rebuild the Final as "Lid Flip"** (map key `CrumbleShelf`, Final mode, 3 → 1 champion).

The floor is a 9 × 9 grid of capsule lids that light up yellow and flip over 1 s later. The pattern speeds up, and the outer ring retires every 25 s down to the inner 3 × 3. A low sweeper turns across the top shelf. A fall lands you on a smaller lower shelf, which is one more chance, and then you're out. The last Rushling standing wins.

This replaces the reactive crumbling-tile grid. Tiles that drop when stepped on are the signature of an existing game's final, so this change is about originality as well as netcode.

Two more reasons it matters:
- **The flip pattern is a pure function of the round's hazard seed.** In M6 that seed becomes the MagicBlock VRF seed, so judges can see the on-chain randomness decide the Final.
- **There's no reactive tile state to replicate.**

## 2. G4.5 layout: Lid Flip (the plan)

Metres; the top shelf lid tops at y 6, the lower shelf lid tops at y 0, kill-Y **−6** (as today, `FinalRules`).

| Part | Build | Variant A / B |
|---|---|---|
| **Top shelf** | `LID_FLIP` grid `cs_lids_top`: **9 × 9 lids of 1.8 m**, gap 0.06 m (pitch 1.86 m, ≈ 16.7 m square), 0.4 m thick, top y 6. Start from `LidGridDef.Default`: lit 30 ticks (1.0 s), flipped 45 ticks, first wave at 90, intervals 90 → 75 → 60 → 45 ticks, 8 waves per stage, 20 → 35 % of the alive lids per wave, **rings retire every 25 s** (750 / 1500 / 2250 ticks) down to the inner 3 × 3, warn 90 ticks, ≥ 5 of the inner 3 × 3 solid at every tick | B: a different stage curve (e.g. intervals 84 → 42, 22 → 35 %), the same retire times |
| **Spawns** | 3 spawns on ring 2 of the top shelf, 120° apart, facing the centre (all solid until the first wave by construction: first wave ≥ 3 s) | — |
| **Top sweeper** | one **low** SPINNER `cs_sweep_top` on a post at the grid centre, bar 0.35 m (jump it), arm ≤ 8.4 m (reaches ring 4), period 120–180 ticks, starting at **20 s** (phase it so the bar is outside the inner 3 × 3 at RUSH, or keep the post centre and let the 3 × 3 be its hub). A hit knocks you, often onto a lit or open lid | B: the other direction |
| **Lower shelf** | `LID_FLIP` grid `cs_lids_low`: **5 × 5 lids of 1.8 m**, top y 0, centred under the top grid. Its own schedule: first wave at 30 s, intervals 75 → 45 ticks, ring 2 retires at 75 s (the inner 3 × 3 stays, ≥ 5 solid). A fall from the top lands here: **one more chance**. A fall from here passes the kill-Y: out | B: offset schedule |
| **Catch** | the 9 × 9 top shelf is wider than the 5 × 5 lower shelf, so a fall from the outer rings can miss it. That's intended (the outer rings are the risky ones), but **show the lower shelf's edge** clearly from above | — |
| **Frame** | the claw-machine frame scaled to the new footprint: dressing only, outside every gameplay volume, never over the shelves where it blocks the camera | — |

Timeline (variant A, at the defaults): RUSH 0 s → waves from 3 s → sweeper from 20 s → ring 4 retires 25 s → ring 3 at 50 s → ring 2 at 75 s (top: inner 3 × 3 only; lower: inner 3 × 3 only) → the 120 s timer. The fairness cap guarantees ≥ 5 solid inner lids on both shelves, so the end comes from the sweeper knocks and the wave speed, not from the floor vanishing. **Tune so a winner almost always emerges before the timer** (§3c).

### G4.5b Bots (≈ 1 h)
`FinalPlan`:
- stand on a lid that stays solid longest. Score each lid by the pure state over the next ~1.5 s: lit or flipped soon = bad, ring retiring within the warn window = bad, inner 3 × 3 = good, crowding = slightly bad;
- move when your lid lights up (a 1.0 s telegraph is enough at 5 m/s for ≈ 2–3 lids);
- hop over open lids, never path across a lit lid that will flip before you're off it;
- **jump the sweeper bar** (`ReactToBars`);
- on the lower shelf, the same logic on the 5 × 5.

Tiers keep their reaction and hesitation differences: normal bots misjudge more, which is what ends rounds.

| Check | Target |
|---|---|

| Fair falls | **100 %** of lid falls were preceded by ≥ 1.0 s of yellow on that lid, or by a sweeper knock in the last 1 s |
| Second chance | ≥ 70 % of first falls from the top land on the lower shelf when they started inside the lower shelf's footprint; players on the lower shelf survive ≥ 8 s on average |
| Inner floor | at every tick ≥ 5 of each grid's inner 3 × 3 solid (asserted in QA as well as in the allowlist) |

Tuning levers, in this order:
1. sweeper period and start time;
2. stage curve (intervals, %);
