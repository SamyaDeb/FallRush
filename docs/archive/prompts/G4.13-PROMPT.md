### B. Bug: Lid Flip floor turns black and looks broken when the lids fold down
The user's words, playing build #4 (soft, High, Chrome): *"in the last round when floor folding down, theres a glitch the floor getting black and somehow broken"*.

**Reproduce it first, in a player build.** Use the Mac player or the saved `client-unity/Builds/WebGL-g412-build4` in Chrome.
- Capture frames during a lid flip (lit → swing down → open → swing back) and during a ring retire (warn → drop away).
- Do it in soft and in candy (F7), and on High and on Low.
- Note when it happens and in which look and level.

Suspects, in order (check them; don't assume):
1. **Shadows on the instanced lids (soft only).**
   - `LidGridView.Draw()` uses `ShadowCastingMode.On` + `receiveShadows` in soft.
   - A lid rotating on its hinge can self-shadow (shadow acne or peter-panning, so it goes black). It can also cast a big black slab onto the lower shelf.
   - The shadow-caster pass or the instancing shadow variant may be stripped in the player (the same class of bug as the G4.12 grade).
   - Test: soft with lid shadows off, and candy (no lid shadows). If both are clean, this is the cause.
2. **The open underside / `Open` batch colour too dark.**
   - The `Open` colour (0.32, 0.24, 0.5) and the mesh's dark-underside vertex colour pass through `GraphicsQuality.AlbedoFor` / `InverseGrade`.
   - In soft that may land near black.
   - Measure the on-screen pixel.
3. **Mesh normals or back faces.**
   - The swung lid shows faces with wrong normals or culled back faces, so it looks broken or has holes.
   - The 6-tick swing interpolation may also pop.
4. **Sorting, z-fighting or gaps between the open lid and the lower shelf / gap geometry.**

Fix the real cause, render only:
- Keep the lid readable. Open = clearly a hole, but a dark *lavender / ink* tone, not pure black.
- Keep the lit / warn telegraph exactly as readable as today.
- If the fix is "no shadows on lids", keep the blob shadows and lower-shelf shading so a fall still reads.

Add a test if the cause can be tested. For example: the open/underside colour after the grade has luma ≥ some floor, or the lid material has a shadow-caster pass that isn't stripped (`ShaderUtil` / variant check).

Commit `fix(g4.13b): Lid Flip lid flip render glitch: <cause>`.
