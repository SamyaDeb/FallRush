## 2. G4.3 layout: Goo Tower

Square spiral, counter-clockwise from the spawn corner, **2 laps × 4 sides = 8 sides (k 0–7)**.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Core | 14 × 14 m (half 7 m), solid, its wall is the inner edge of the ledge |
| Ledge | 5 m wide (band 7…12 m from the centre, centre line 9.5 m); two 2.5 m lanes where a side has lanes |
| Sides | straight part 14 m between 5 × 5 m corner landings; rise **2.75 m per side** (y 0 → 22 m) |
| Path | ≈ 8 × 19 m ≈ 152 m + summit ≈ 160 m |
| Falls | lap 2 sits 11 m above lap 1, so a fall from lap 2 lands on the same side one lap down; a fall from lap 1 lands on the ground ring at y 0 |
| Rails | **lap 1: 0.4 m outer rail** (you get bounced, rarely thrown off); **lap 2: no outer rail** |

Every lane of every side carries a **knock hazard** (hits or door push-outs count, falls don't), so no route is free. Hazard ids are stable: `gt_k{side}_{kind}_{n}`.

| Side | Lap · y | Build | Variant A / B |
|---|---|---|---|
| **k0 Stair Steps** | 1 · 0 → 2.75 | inner lane: 8 steps of 0.34 m; outer lane: 4 FALLING_BLOCKs (2.4 m, gaps ≤ 1.6 m, drop 0.6 s after landing, back after 4 s); **two MALLETs swinging *along* the side**, one per lane, at s −2.5 / +2.5 | mallet periods |
| **k1 Lift Pair** | 1 · 2.75 → 5.5 | a 2.75 m wall at s +2; two 3 × 3 m lifts (LoopPlatform, bottom → top), periods offset by half so one is always near the bottom; **a ROLLING_BALL crossing the approach** (outward across the ledge, toward the rail) at s −3 | lift periods / ball direction |

| Check | Target |
|---|---|
| FREE ROUTES | **0** |
| Route cost per side | slowest valid route **1.00–1.25×** the fastest (single-route sides exempt) |
| Hits per route (normal bots) | ≥ 10 and ≤ 300 per 100 traversals; no side where normal bots average > 1.5 falls per traversal |
| Goo margin | every route index escapes **alone** at reference speed with a **min goo margin of 8–10 s**; the goo never overtakes the slowest valid route |
| Escape | **exactly 5 of 8** in every run; 0 soft-locks |
| Normal-bot median | **90–110 s** |
| Reference bots | ≤ 5 falls per round on average |
| Determinism | same run seed → same result |

Tuning levers, in this order:
1. goo start rate and delay;
2. lift and door timings;
3. mallet / sweeper periods;
4. belt speeds;
5. gap widths within the movement-v2 rules;
6. bumper positions.

Don't change movement or the escape rule. Keep every tuning step in a before → after table.
